Actuarial Formulas & Theoretical Framework

1. Claim Frequency (Poisson GLM with Log-Exposure Offset)

$$\log(E[N_i]) = \mathbf{X}_i \boldsymbol{\beta} + \log(\text{Exposure}_i)$$ Annualized claim rate: $\lambda_i = \exp(\mathbf{X}_i \boldsymbol{\beta})$ (Exposure = 1.0 year).
Expected claims for term: $\hat{N}_i = \lambda_i \times \text{Exposure}_i$.

2. Claim Severity (Gamma GLM with Log Link)

$$\log(E[Y_i \mid N_i > 0]) = \mathbf{X}_i \boldsymbol{\gamma} \implies \hat{Y}_i = \exp(\mathbf{X}_i \boldsymbol{\gamma})$$ Models expected cost per individual claim, accounting for skewed, positive claim distributions.

3. Pure Premium & Term Expected Loss

$$\text{Annual Pure Premium} = \lambda_i \times \hat{Y}_i$$ $$\text{Expected Loss for Policy Term} = \lambda_i \times \hat{Y}_i \times \text{Exposure}_i$$ Exposure is strictly preserved and never double-counted.

4. Commercial Loading Formula

$$\text{Commercial Premium} = \frac{\text{Expected Loss} + \text{Fixed Expense}}{1 - \text{VarExp} - \text{RiskMargin} - \text{ProfitMargin}}$$ Strictly validated: $1 - (\text{VarExp} + \text{RiskMargin} + \text{ProfitMargin}) > 0.05$.
